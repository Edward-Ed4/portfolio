Pig Disease Detection System

An innovative IoT and Machine Learning solution that helps farmers detect pig diseases early through automated monitoring, improving livestock health management and reducing losses.

Technologies Used

Project Overview

The Pig Disease Detection System represents a convergence of IoT hardware and artificial intelligence to address a critical challenge in modern farming: early disease detection in livestock. Traditional methods require constant manual inspection, which is time-consuming and often leads to late detection.

This system automates the monitoring process using an ESP32-CAM module that captures images of pigs at regular intervals. These images are processed by a machine learning model trained to recognize signs of common pig diseases through visual symptoms like skin discoloration, behavioral changes, and physical abnormalities.

The ML model runs on the edge device itself using TensorFlow Lite, enabling fast inference without requiring constant internet connectivity. When a potential health issue is detected, the system immediately sends an alert to the farmer's mobile device through a Flutter-based mobile application.

By providing early warnings, farmers can take swift action to isolate affected animals, consult veterinarians, and prevent disease spread across their livestock. This not only saves animals but also reduces economic losses and improves overall farm management efficiency.
